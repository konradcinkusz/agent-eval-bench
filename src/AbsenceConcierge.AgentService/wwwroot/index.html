Absence Concierge

Tell it you are ill. It resolves the dates in your timezone, fetches the leave types, checks your existing bookings, drafts the request — and then stops.

Everything below runs against a fictional company on in-memory fixtures. No real data, no account, nothing is submitted anywhere.

    Live model replies

    By default the replies are written by a deterministic composer — no model, no network, no cost. With an access code, a small model rewrites the same reply into better prose. It cannot change a date, a decision, or whether the request was submitted: it runs after every step has already decided, and its output is discarded if it drifts from the grounded reply.
